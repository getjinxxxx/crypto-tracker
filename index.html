Simple vs Advanced view

Use the Simple toggle at the top for everyday checking. It shows just the essentials: how much you invested, what it’s worth now, and your profit or loss.

Switch to Advanced when you want the extra tools: charts you can change (line/candles), a market snapshot (RSI, trend), a portfolio goal, history graph, what-if calculator, sorting, and more.

Example: Every morning, open Simple view to see “You are up ₱300 (+5%)” at a glance. When you’re deciding whether to buy more, switch to Advanced to check the chart and market snapshot.

The basics

Entry price — the price of the coin when you bought it. Example: you bought BTC at ₱4,900,000 — that’s your entry price.
Invested (₱) — the peso amount you put in. This is the only box you type in. Example: you put in ₱2,500 for that buy.
Amount — how much of the coin you got (Invested ÷ Entry price). Example: ₱2,500 ÷ ₱4,900,000 = 0.00051 BTC.
Current (₱) — what your coins are worth right now at today’s price.
P/L (profit / loss) — Current minus Invested. Green = earning, red = losing (for now). Example: worth ₱2,700 now, you invested ₱2,500 → +₱200 profit.

Active vs Pending

Active — coins you already bought (your real holdings).
Pending — a waiting order: you set a target price and it buys automatically if the price drops there. Example: BTC is ₱4.9M now; you set a pending buy at ₱4.5M and wait for a dip.

Common terms

Dip — when the price drops. Many people like to buy on dips (cheaper).
DCA (peso-cost averaging) — buying a little at a time instead of all at once, so your average price smooths out.
Average buy price — your overall entry across all buys. If the market is above it, you’re in profit.
Break-even — the price where you’re neither up nor down. Shown as a gold dashed line on the chart.
Take profit — selling (some or all) while you’re up, to lock in gains.
Volatile — prices move up and down a lot. Crypto is very volatile.

Advanced tools

Line vs Candles chart — Line shows the overall trend simply. Candles show each period’s open/high/low/close (more detail). Green candle = went up, red = went down.
RSI — a momentum meter (0–100). Below 30 = “oversold” (fell a lot), above 70 = “overbought” (rose a lot). Just a guide.
Market snapshot — combines RSI + range position + trend into a simple read (Looks low / Neutral / Looks high). Not advice — just context.
What-if calculator — type a future price to see how much profit you’d have if it gets there.
Reminder: everything here is a helper based on price data, not financial advice. Crypto is risky — only invest what you can afford to lose, and the decision is always yours.
